# ScuffIQ Partner Technician Agreement

## 1. Who this agreement is between

1.1 This agreement is between **ScuffIQ Ltd** (company number 17478888), registered office 82a James Carter Road, Bury St. Edmunds, IP28 7DE ("ScuffIQ"), and the sole trader or company named in the schedule ("you", the "Technician").

1.2 ScuffIQ sells mobile smart repair services to customers through its website. ScuffIQ takes the enquiry, prepares the quote, manages bookings and deposits, and communicates with the customer. ScuffIQ offers jobs to you, and you carry out the work.

1.3 You are an independent business. You are not an employee, worker or agent of ScuffIQ.

---

## 2. What this agreement covers

2.1 This agreement sets out how jobs are offered and accepted, how you are paid, the standards of work we expect, and what each of us is responsible for.

2.2 Nothing in this agreement requires you to accept any job. You may decline any job offer without penalty.

---

## 3. How jobs are offered and accepted

3.1 When a job is available in your area, ScuffIQ will send you the job details, including the customer's first name, vehicle, location, service required, and photos.

3.2 You will then confirm the **price** and your **availability** through the link we send you. This confirmation is your offer to do the job at the price you have stated, on the date and time you have confirmed.

3.3 ScuffIQ will confirm the job in writing. Once ScuffIQ confirms the job, a binding agreement exists between you and ScuffIQ for that job, on the terms of this agreement.

3.4 If you do not respond to an offer, it lapses. ScuffIQ may offer the job to another technician.

---

4.2 **Commission.** ScuffIQ keeps a commission of [●]% of the confirmed price, plus VAT where applicable, for introducing and managing the job. This is shown on each job before you confirm it.

4.5 **VAT.** [You are / are not] registered for VAT. If you are, you must invoice ScuffIQ with VAT and account for it. ScuffIQ will pay the VAT on top of the commission [or as agreed].

4.6 **Invoices.** You will send an invoice for each completed job, or ScuffIQ may issue a self-billing invoice on your behalf if you agree in writing.

---

## 5. The customer relationship

5.1 ScuffIQ manages the relationship with the customer. This includes quotes, bookings, deposits, reminders and customer communications.

5.2 You must not contact customers introduced through ScuffIQ to arrange or take work outside ScuffIQ, for [12] months after the job. Repeat or direct business with a customer must go through ScuffIQ.

5.3 You may contact the customer about the job itself, such as arrival time, access and the repair.

---

## 6. Standard of work

6.1 You will carry out each job with reasonable care and skill, in line with good practice in smart repair.

6.2 You will complete each job within the time agreed, and inform ScuffIQ promptly of any delay.

6.3 You will use materials of a good quality and suitable for the repair.

6.4 Where a repair is defective, you will put it right at no extra cost to the customer, within [3] months of the job, unless the defect was caused by the customer or by circumstances beyond your control.

6.5 You will tell ScuffIQ before the job if you think the damage is not suitable for smart repair. You will not be penalised for declining an unsuitable job.

---

## 7. Cancellations and no-shows

7.1 If you need to cancel a confirmed job, you must tell ScuffIQ as early as possible, and at least [48] hours before the appointment where you can.

7.2 If you cancel with less notice, or do not attend, ScuffIQ may [withhold part of the commission / refuse to offer you further jobs for [●] days]. This does not apply where the cancellation is caused by ScuffIQ, the customer or circumstances beyond your control.

7.3 If the customer cancels with less than [48] hours' notice, ScuffIQ will tell you, and [you will be paid [●]% of the job price / the job will be treated as cancelled].

---

## 8. Insurance

8.1 You must hold, throughout this agreement:
- **Public liability insurance** of at least £[●] for each claim, and
- [Professional indemnity insurance of at least £[●]].

8.2 You must give ScuffIQ a copy of your insurance certificate before your first job, and again each time it renews.

8.3 ScuffIQ does not insure you. Your insurance is your own responsibility.

## 9. Responsibility and liability

9.1 Each party is responsible for its own acts and omissions.

9.2 You are responsible for any loss or damage caused to a customer's vehicle by your negligence or breach of this agreement.

9.3 ScuffIQ is responsible for its own acts and omissions, including the quote, booking and payment process.

9.4 Nothing in this agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any other liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

9.5 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, such as lost profit, unless caused by fraud.

---

## 10. You are self-employed

10.1 You are free to decide how and when you work, provided you meet the agreed job times.

10.2 You provide your own tools, equipment, transport and insurance.

10.3 You are responsible for your own tax, National Insurance and VAT. ScuffIQ will not deduct tax or National Insurance from your payments.

10.4 You may take work from other businesses, provided it does not conflict with a confirmed ScuffIQ job or clause 5.

10.5 You must carry out confirmed jobs yourself. You may send a substitute only with ScuffIQ's written agreement, and the substitute must be insured and suitably skilled. [Solicitor to review: a right to substitute supports self-employed status, but needs to be real.]

---

## 11. Customer data

11.1 You will use customer information only for the purpose of carrying out the job.

11.2 You will keep customer information secure, and delete it once the job and any warranty period have ended, unless the law requires you to keep it longer.

11.3 ScuffIQ is the data controller for customer information it collects. You act on ScuffIQ's instructions when using that information for a job, and you must comply with UK data protection law.

---

## 12. Complaints

12.1 If a customer complains about your work, you will co-operate with ScuffIQ to resolve it, and respond within [2] working days.

12.2 ScuffIQ may decide how a complaint is resolved, including refunds, after speaking with you.

---

## 13. Ending this agreement

13.1 Either party may end this agreement by giving [14] days' written notice. Confirmed jobs must still be completed or handed over in an orderly way.

13.2 ScuffIQ may end this agreement immediately if you:
- commit a serious breach that is not fixed within [7] days of notice,
- are in serious or repeated breach of clause 6, 8 or 11, or
- act dishonestly towards a customer or ScuffIQ.

13.3 Clauses 4 (payment for completed jobs), 5.2, 9, 11 and 14 survive the end of this agreement.

---

## 14. Confidentiality

14.1 You will keep ScuffIQ's pricing, commission arrangements, customer lists and business information confidential, and use them only for this agreement.

---

## 15. General

15.1 **Changes.** Changes to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.

15.2 **Whole agreement.** This agreement, with any schedule, is the whole agreement between us about its subject matter.

15.3 **Notices.** Notices must be sent by email to the addresses on the schedule.

15.4 **Law.** This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
